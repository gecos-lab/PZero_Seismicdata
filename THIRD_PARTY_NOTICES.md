# Sources and licensing

## F3 data

The original survey declaration,
[`wells/source_inputs/.survey`](wells/source_inputs/.survey), identifies
**F3 Demo 2023** as licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
The [TerraNubis F3 page](https://terranubis.com/datainfo/F3-Demo-2023) lists the
licence and contributors:

- Original seismic: **NAM**; original source: **NLOG**.
- OpendTect project, derived attribute volumes and reference horizons:
  **dGB Earth Sciences**.

We thank dGB Earth Sciences for making the F3 data available as an OpendTect
project through TerraNubis.

Bundled adaptations include PZero interpretations, converted wells,
coordinate-aligned surfaces and tetrahedral meshes. Their methods and changes
are documented in [coordinates](docs/coordinates.md) and
[provenance](metadata/provenance.json). These F3-derived data retain CC BY-SA
3.0: preserve attribution and the licence link, identify modifications, and
share adaptations under the same licence. No provider endorsement is implied.
